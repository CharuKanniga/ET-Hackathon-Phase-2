# 🌍 Domain-Specialized GenAI Agent for ESG & Climate Compliance

## 📌 Overview

Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) face increasing pressure to comply with ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) and climate regulations. However, ESG reporting and compliance processes are complex, expensive, and time-consuming for small businesses.

This project presents a **Domain-Specialized Generative AI Agent** that helps SMEs automate ESG compliance, calculate ESG scores, estimate carbon emissions, detect compliance risk, and generate sustainability insights.

The system acts as an **AI Compliance Copilot** that simplifies sustainability reporting and helps businesses become ESG-compliant and investment-ready.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

SMEs face several challenges in ESG compliance:

* Complex ESG and BRSR regulations
* Manual and time-consuming report preparation
* High cost of ESG consultants
* Risk of compliance penalties
* Lack of automated compliance tools

There is a clear gap between **regulatory requirements** and **SMEs' ability to comply**.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

We developed a **Domain-Specialized AI Agent** that assists companies in ESG and climate compliance through automation and intelligent analysis.

### The system can:

* 🤖 Answer ESG-related questions using an AI assistant
* 📊 Calculate ESG score based on company data
* 🌱 Estimate carbon emissions
* ⚠️ Detect compliance risk level
* 📝 Generate sustainability suggestions
* 📑 Support ESG and BRSR compliance reporting

---

## ⚙️ System Architecture

The system consists of the following components:

* User Interface (Web Application)
* AI ESG Assistant
* ESG Scoring Engine
* Carbon Emission Calculator
* Compliance Risk Detection Module
* Report Generation Module
* ESG Policy Knowledge Base

The AI agent retrieves ESG policies, analyzes company data, calculates sustainability metrics, and generates compliance insights.

## 📊 ESG Score Model

The ESG score is calculated based on sustainability factors such as:

* Electricity Usage
* Fuel Usage
* Water Usage
* Waste Production
* Employee Welfare

The system generates an ESG score out of 100 and classifies compliance risk as **Low, Medium, or High**.
